Help / Learn

Learn

Plain-English guides to everything this app does. No coding needed — read a card, understand a concept, then go run it for real.

Start here
The four ideas the whole app is built on
Concept

What is a fingerprint?

A fingerprint is a short code the app makes from each page’s text — like a name tag for the words. Two pages with almost the same fingerprint are saying almost the same thing. That is how the app spots near-duplicate content you may not want competing with itself.

Concept

What is authority flow?

When one page links to another, it passes along a little of its trust — we call that authority. Strong pages that link to a page lift it up. This view shows where your trust pools and where it leaks, so you can point your strongest pages at the ones that need help.

Concept

What is the autotuner?

The app weighs many signals to decide which link suggestions matter most. The autotuner is the assistant that nudges those weights for you, learning from what actually worked. You stay in charge — it proposes, you approve. Nothing changes without your yes.

Safety

How gates protect you

A gate is an automatic checkpoint. Before any change reaches your live site, it has to pass tests, evidence checks, and policy rules. If something looks wrong, the gate stops it and tells you why in plain words. You never have to catch mistakes by hand.
